Nexira — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Nexira may process Discord-related information required for its functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Server configuration data
  • Command usage and technical information

2. Why We Use This Information

Information is used only to provide and maintain the Bot's features, store server settings, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Sharing

Nexira does not sell users' personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law or when necessary to operate essential services used by the Bot.

4. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in the Bot's database for as long as necessary to provide its functionality.

5. Data Removal

Users or server administrators may request deletion of stored data by contacting the Nexira developer through the official support server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information, although no system can guarantee complete security.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot or its functionality changes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the Nexira developer through the official support server.
